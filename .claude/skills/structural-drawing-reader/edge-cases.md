# Edge cases: sign, action, record

Find the row by its sign, do what it says, record as it says. Each row ends in a claim, an
`unread` with its question, or a question; none ends in a chosen value. Basis column: where the
case was met or sourced (`pilot` = RTI-0014 pilot on a two-block factory set; `audit` =
wave-5 audits D and E; `cad` = `dwg-lab/references/04-cad/NOTES.md`; `conv` =
`docs/research/vn-structural-drawing-conventions.md`). A case with no row: describe it in the
part's `notes.md` as `not met`, ask, and name it in the hand-back so it can become a row.

## The set and its sheets

| Id | Sign | Do | Record | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E01 | Two sheet-number series, two building names in titles, or the same plan title twice with different content, in one file | Treat as several blocks until the engineer says otherwise: one tree branch per block, sheets assigned by title words and number series, assignment `low`. Rival reading to rule out: one building in two versions. When the tool reports one grid system across both, say that the blocks need separate placement and no tool does it. | `reader_breakdown_confirm` shows the split; K1 `question` until answered | pilot |
| E02 | One sheet number on two sheets, or a number from the other block's series | Place neither by number. Compare titles, marks and position in the file; state the two readings (mis-numbered sheet, sheet of the other block). | Breakdown row `unplaced`; in the manifest the sheet sits in the part its title names, the question in that part's `producer_questions`; one blocking question | pilot |
| E03 | The drawing list's own title names one discipline or one block | The list covers that part only. Other parts have `no list`: their completeness cannot be checked. | C1 row per part; question per uncovered part | pilot |
| E04 | A load-path link has no sheet (steel shown only to the column bases; no roof; no superstructure) | Search titles and headings for the link's words first. Then: a discipline not sent, not yet issued, or in another file. A table that lists members no sheet shows is evidence the part exists. | blocking `reader_scope_drawings_missing` with the search record; node coverage `absent`; the part's rows `absent` with that question | pilot |
| E05 | Probe lists layouts; title blocks sit in paper space, members in model space | Sheet identity (number, revision, scale) may come from a layout while the content is a model-space window. Use the detector's sheets; a file whose sheets were not detected is `unread` for phases 1–2. | Reach `no sheet`; the producer's `sheets_not_detected` question | cad |
| E06 | Several scales in one file: details drawn larger, a dimension text that differs from its measured length, a title-block scale | The printed dimension text is what the drawing says; a typed-over or scaled dimension is a conflict. The title-block scale proves nothing about model-space geometry. A size from `contents` is the real size only while the plan's grid spacing in `grid-systems.json` is dimension-backed (`spacing_evidence` status `dimension_agrees`, or `dimension_measured` with a whole-millimetre spacing); otherwise the size is asked. Before taking any size from a detail or section run `dimensions --sheet <S>`: only a row with `override_value` or `expected_text_value` gives a ratio (that value ÷ `measured`); a `text_rule: measured` row confirms none (it prints its measurement × DIMLFAC, not reported: T26 proposed). A ratio on the detail: size = measured × ratio, recorded with both sources. No ratio: a linework size is recorded only with an agreeing printed dimension (a text entry), else asked (printed dimensions are always recorded); no dimension rows, the size is `unread` (E39). | cite `dimension_override_differs`; the ratio in the note | cad |
| E07 | Probe lists xrefs, or layers named `<file>\|<layer>`; a plan with members but no grid or background | The grid may live in a file that was not sent. | `unread: xref <name> not resolved`; question asking for the file | cad |
| E08 | A sheet with a title but almost no other text; read-log warns of inserts not exploded | Run `geometry` on it (phase 0). Geometry present: the sheet is readable for linework (layers, groups), `unread` for text. Content inside a viewport or an unexpanded insert (`transform:VIEWPORT`) stays unread. | Reach `near-empty` only with no geometry either; node coverage `unread`; one question naming the sheets | pilot |
| E09 | A note or title says symmetric, mirrored, similar, opposite hand; a plan drawn in halves; a view rotated against the key plan | The drawn half is `seen`; the other half is a claim resting on the note, `single_source`. Halves of one grid are named together and never selected as two grids. A rotated view's axes are compared by label, never by direction on the sheet. | finding citing the note entry; question when the note's scope is unclear | audit |
| E10 | Text outside every frame; a table that runs past its frame (rows below or beside a schedule's last frame line) | Cluster it and assign it by position to the nearest frame it continues (reading-method § Phase 0 item 5) before any `no rows`, `schedule unread` or `absent`. Read the rows as that sheet's, say `by position; no entry ties it to the sheet`; no entry id is a sheet id. A cluster no frame continues stays unassigned. | Reach rows `U<n>`; the `values.json` rows cite the entry ids; question only for the unassigned remainder | pilot |
| E11 | A title-block title names a subject no heading on the sheet shows | Search the sheet for the subject word before saying so. A copied title block is the usual cause. Class the sheet by its headings. | `cross_check` finding citing both entries, with its question | audit |
| E12 | A sheet present in two revisions; revision clouds or triangles; a `superseded` stamp; dates that differ within one series | Ask which is current; take no value from either until answered. Clouds and triangles are not detected by any tool: say so. | blocking question; C10 | checklist A3–A4 |
| E13 | Issue status reads tender, preliminary, for approval | Every value is provisional whatever its confidence. | one non-blocking question per set; stated in K4 | checklist A4 |
| E14 | Title block or layers of another discipline (architectural columns, walls) inside a structural sheet | Layer names are hints. A member drawn by another discipline is not a structural member until a structural sheet marks it. | `--note` on the finding; question when it changes a count | cad |

## Text

| Id | Sign | Do | Record | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E15 | `blocked_text_count` above 0 on a sheet you need (notes, schedule header, title) | SKILL.md § Blocked text. The topic is `unread`, never `absent`. One font may block most of a set: name the font question once and count per sheet. | Reach row; phase 2 topics `unread (N, Q-font)` | audit |
| E16 | Entries that do not print (frozen, off or no-plot layer; invisible attribute) | Drawing content is what prints. A value found only there is quoted `không in (T…)`. An off layer may hold a superseded design. A member carrying the invisible flag (group 60) is counted by `geometry`/`contents` and not drawn by `render`: render its region; drawn there by other entities, the count stands (`counted from hidden outlines, confirmed by render`); nothing drawn, it is not counted and the count is recorded. Say only what the tool's warning says: mostly hidden visibility states of dynamic blocks, not drawn by `render`. | `unread`; question when it would change a value or the render does not settle it | cad, audit |
| E17 | Bilingual text, or one term in two spellings | Both spellings go into `searched:`. A claim cites the entry as written. | search record | pilot |
| E18 | A glyph that may be a diameter sign, a degree sign or a legacy-font letter | Quote as stored. The reading comes from the callouts producer or the legend. | cite the producer's question | conv |

## Values

| Id | Sign | Do | Record | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E19 | Header units disagree with dimension magnitudes; a length written with a unit suffix (`m`) beside bare numbers | Units are the engineer's answer per file. Quote the suffixed value as evidence in the units question. Levels in metres beside dimensions in millimetres is common practice, verified only for architectural sets: state both, assume neither. | evidence on `units_confirm_all` | conv 3.2 |
| E20 | `±0.000` printed with an absolute level; two sheets giving different pairs | The pair is the datum claim, per block. Structural and architectural datums may differ. | level finding; blocking question on a difference | conv 3.2, pilot |
| E21 | A floor level with no word saying finished or structural | Keep it in the block's level question; the offset between the two is never assumed. | question | conv 3.2 |
| E22 | Many level texts on one sheet; levels on sections of steps, pits, platforms, canopies, beam soffits | Storey or reference levels come from plan titles and the level schedule; the rest are local. Dense repeats at one place are noise until a heading explains them. | candidates listed verbatim in the storey question | audit |
| E23 | Pile sheets: toe level, cut-off level, head embedment into the cap, test piles, pile length, pile count in a title | Cut-off level and cap bottom level are two values; quote each where printed. Count from the plan title is a printed value; the count of pile symbols is the `hand sum` of the per-type measured findings' `symbols` × `count` (method § Phase 4 step 5), checked against `contents` `symbols.total` (unique centres, `outside` 0). A pile designation is quoted as written; its class letters and numbers are read from the project's pile table. | findings per value; C5 for the two counts; question on a difference | `pilot; steel-industrial-building-drawings.md §5 (unverified)` |
| E24 | Legend value against a tag on the same sheet; notes against a schedule; plan against detail | Neither overrules the other. | `cross_check` finding citing both, with its question | audit |
| E25 | A typical detail, `TYP.`, `U.N.O.`, `tương tự` | It supplies values to every member in its stated scope. No stated scope → question. A member with its own detail keeps its own values. | the member's rows cite the typical or U.N.O. entry | checklist B1 |
| E26 | An engineer answer that entries seem to contradict | Only allowed, printed entries stating another value for the same thing contradict it. Search the answered value first; a hit on the same span means no contradiction. | blocking question quoting the answer id and entry ids | audit |
| E27 | A cited standard edition is superseded, or has no year | Quote verbatim; compare with `docs/research/vn-structural-standards-editions.md`. | non-blocking question; never mapped | research |

## Grids and marks

| Id | Sign | Do | Record | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E28 | Grid questions raised on a 3D view, a key plan or a detail's local axes | Say what the sheet is, citing its title entry; such axes are no grid fault and no grid candidate. | evidence on the producer's question | audit |
| E29 | A plan sheet with no grid system; a system missing labels its sibling sheet has; an unlabelled axis line | The tool not finding a grid is `unread`. Search the sheet for axis labels and dimension texts before any statement. Grids are never rebuilt from member positions or averaged. | question with the labels and dimensions found | audit |
| E30 | Sub-axes (`1'`, `A'`, `1a`, `1/1`), skipped letters, skewed or radial axes | Read the bubbles; which direction carries letters is read, never assumed. | cite the producer's question | conv 3.1 |
| E31 | A short label next to a section cut, a bar number in a circle, a sheet code, a profile letter | It is not a member family until a schedule or heading uses it as one: conventions.md § Collisions. | the prefix question, `đề xuất (chưa xác nhận)` | audit |
| E32 | `matched: 0`, an empty `plan_only`, no schedule tables, though the sheets show tables; a column or rebar table (headed by a mark, section, bar diameter or quantity column) that `marks` does not list | The table was not recognised: `marks` misses column and rebar tables. Read it by rows from the entries; each row you cite is a `values.json` row (`count_schedule`, `size`) with its entry ids; say `chưa so sánh` for the rest. | tool gap in `notes.md`; question listing the table headers by entry id | pilot, audit, rev11 H |
| E33 | Plan marks and schedule marks differ by a suffix (`Z4` on plan, `Z4.1` and `Z4.2` in the table) or by grouping (plan `K-5`, `K-6`, `K-7`, schedule one row `K-5÷7`) | Two or three readings: sub-types or groups the other sheet does not distinguish, a renumbering, a plan or table not updated. Quote counts mark by mark for each; a hand sum only with addends (C12). | question; C4, C5 and C12 rows | pilot |
| E34 | A bill of materials or quantity table beside the drawings | It is a cross-check source, never a geometry source. | C5 | pilot |
| E35 | A work folder already holds reader findings or questions and no `W/reading-record.md` | Phase 0 lists them (reading-method § Recording forms, last item); cite what still holds, withdraw only what your reading of the same entries contradicts. A `reader_breakdown_confirm` amends an older block-layout question. In part mode re-ask each open question you keep with `ask --part scout` (same code, blocking flag, file and `--where`: the part is added) and list its id in the `producer_questions` of every part it concerns; withdraw the rest. | the scout's `counts:` line | rev8 run |
| E36 | `marks` reports a match for a letter-digit label that is a dimension letter (`V1`, `V2` on a purlin detail), a weld size, a bar number or a section label | Name the false match in `notes.md`; it is no family until a schedule or heading uses it (E31). Cite the entries that show what the label is. | `notes.md` line; tool gap | review D-1-05 |
| E37 | One grid system labelled wider (more axes, a larger overall dimension) than the members drawn on it, or two blocks on identical labels | C11: take the axes that carry members from `contents` on every plan of the block (method § C11 procedure). A grid wider than the members, or members only on part of it, is a blocking question with both readings (grid right and block partly drawn; grid copied from the other block). Read the dimensions before saying so; never shorten or rebuild the grid (E29). | `cross_check` finding; blocking question per block | compare #15 |
| E38 | A beam, cap or slab size written `25x50`, `hs=150`, `D400` with no unit beside it | A size is `seen` as printed. Its unit is the file's units answer (E19); with none, quote the figure and ask. A size another quantity implies (bar length, stirrup width) is evidence in the question, never the size. | `size` row `seen` with entry ids, or `asked`; question | compare #26, #28 |
| E39 | A member drawn as loose lines, hatch only or two parallel lines (beams); a detail only in paper space; a section with no dimension row; `marks_unassigned` marks with no outline | The linework tools give no outline, count or size here: read by text (mark, schedule, printed size). The count and the size are `unread (no outline tool)`, never `0`; `render` refuses paper space. C11 by mark position (method § C11) for members with marks but no outline. | rows `unread`; one question per family; `notes.md` `unread` line | rev 10 |
| E40 | `manifest-check` warns `linework_duplicate`; or two blocks' plans hold the same outline sizes and counts at the same offsets; or `contents --dims`, run with E42's `--tol`, shows `printed_dims.disagree` above 0 on a row | The linework of one block may be a copy of another's while its dimension texts were retyped. A size or count from it needs a mark and an agreeing typed dimension on this sheet (`printed_dims[].entry_id`; an automatic dimension prints the geometry itself and has no entry id). None → the row is `unread`; one question to the designer names the copy and both blocks. The two blocks' values are never merged or carried across. | row `unread` with its question; the warning acknowledged in the manifest `acks[]` by that question | rev11 H |
| E41 | A sheet's number series puts it in one block while its grid origin or its grid labels fit another (a sheet of a second building placed on the first building's plan) | Place it by neither alone. Quote the number series, the title entries and the grid origin each side supports; the manifest `origin_note` of both blocks says where the origin sits. Ask which block owns it; until answered the sheet stays in the part its title block names, and the question goes in that part's `producer_questions` (E02). | warning `grid_origin_other_block` acknowledged by the question id; sheet placed in the part its title block names, the question in that part's `producer_questions`; Breakdown row `unplaced` until answered | rev11 H |
| E42 | Outlines of one type differ by a few millimetres (398 against 400, 1498 against 1500), or a drawn size differs slightly from its printed dimension | A drawn size within 5 mm or 1 % (the larger) of its printed dimension, or outlines of one type within that spread, is noise: no question; the printed value is the value; say the spread in the finding `--note`. Before reading two rows as two types, rerun `contents` with `--tol` = the larger of 5 mm and 1 % of the longer side, in the file's answered unit (5 mm is `--tol 5` in mm, `0.5` in cm, `0.005` in m). A spread beyond that is not noise: ask. | no question for the spread; `--note: spread <min>–<max>` | rev11 H |
| E43 | A schedule lists rows (marks, quantities) for a member whose layout sheet is listed in the drawing list but is not in the files | The rows are evidence the member exists (E04); no plan count can be compared. Link each row to the missing sheet by its list entry; say `unread (no plan)` for the count, never `0`. | rows `count_schedule` `seen`, `count_plan` `unread`; one question naming the listed sheet and the schedule rows | rev11 H |
