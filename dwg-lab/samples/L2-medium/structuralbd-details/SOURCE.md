## What it contains

Typical detail sheets from the site's three sections. RC (imperial/English units): column reinforcement detail, beam reinforcement and layout, combined footing detail, pile cap reinforcement and layout (18 in dia). Steel (metric): base plate detail for steel column, steel member connection detail. All files are AC1021 (AutoCAD 2007 format). Content description comes from the page's section headings only; per source page, not verified (DWG not opened).

## Why it is useful

Realistic single-discipline detail sheets: RC bar arrangements and callouts (column/beam/footing/pile cap), steel connection and base-plate detail with plate/bolt/weld notes. Exercises detail-level reading (section cuts, rebar callouts, weld/bolt symbols, mixed imperial/metric). Whether the files contain MTEXT, dimensions or SHX fonts is unknown until opened in the reader.
